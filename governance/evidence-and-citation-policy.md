# Evidence Grades and Citation Policy

## Evidence grades

- **Primary:** a primary publication, dataset, or assembly record, inspected directly, with a stable identifier (DOI, PMID, accession, or release version).
- **Derived:** an analysis or audit in this repository that names its primary inputs, methods, and versions, and can be re-run or re-checked from them.
- **Conjecture:** a historical or architectural proposal. Labelled as such wherever it appears.
- **Unverified:** a source, supplement, or count that has been cited but not inspected. Unverified material may be listed as a lead; it may not be used as evidence for a claim.

## Citation rules

- Cite the original primary publication, not a secondary summary, for any empirical claim.
- Name the source and link the original wherever a statistic, count, or finding is quoted.
- Record inaccessible or unverified supplements explicitly, as the R7 and R8 audits do.
- Historical revisions keep their original citations; corrections are made by a new version or a dated note, not by silently rewriting a historical file.

## Claim discipline

- Present function does not establish original function or origin date.
- Family similarity does not establish orthologous insertion.
- An assembly gap does not establish absence.
- No comparative parity or superiority claim may be made without a quantitative, preregistered comparison under the charter scoring rules.
